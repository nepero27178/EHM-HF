Gutzwiller method:
- Expansion of HF variational approach
- The variational wavefunction is an HF wavefunction suitably projected through combinations of Gutzwiller projectors P, which eliminate all configurations with doubly occupied sites
- Imposes Mott effects by tuning the strength of the Gutzwiller projection
- Variational method: the Gutzwiller wavefunction is optimised

DMFT:
- Approximation of the a lattice problem with many DoF with a single-site effective problem with less DoF
- The dynamics at a given site can be thought of as the interaction of the DoF at this site with an external bath created by all other degrees of freedom on other sites
- Classical MFT: prototypical Weiss mean field to classical Ising model with ferromagnetic exchange
- Curie-Weiss: as dimensionality grows, the solution becomes exact
- DMFT: self-consistent iterative cycle involving the bath Green's function, the local Green's function and the self-energy
- Dynamical nature: it describes the non-static properties

Cluster DMFT:
- Extension to clusters of sites
- Example: Hubbard square

- Ground-states of quadratic hamiltonians
- All coherent states are gaussian
- Wigner quasiprobability distribution (quasi-wavefunction) is gaussian
